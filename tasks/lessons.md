# Lessons

## 2026-11-18: a listed colour is not a mandate to theme everything in it

Munish listed "green" among must-haves for the portfolio redesign. I made green the whole identity (paper, ground, ink) in both mockups. On seeing it he rejected green outright: in cinema it reads as sickly and grotesque.

Rule: when a brief names a single colour, show it as one option at swatch scale (or one hero frame) and get a reaction before building a full mockup around it. Check a colour's meaning in the subject's own world (here, film colour language) before committing to it.

Current direction: no green anywhere. Off-white ground, black-and-white clapper, several vibrant colours used per stage, fine low-intensity grain like andrewsbodega.com.
